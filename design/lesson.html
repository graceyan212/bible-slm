✦ The Rescuer · Story 4

The Brave Shepherd Boy

Once there was a shepherd boy named David — the youngest and smallest in his whole family. But this story isn't really about how brave David was. It's about how big and how faithful God is.

God's people were shaking with fear in front of a giant named Goliath, and not one strong soldier would face him. So God chose the least-likely person of all — a shepherd boy with a sling — to rescue them, so everyone could see the victory was God's, not a strong man's.

With one small stone, down came the giant. God saved his whole people through one young shepherd who stood in their place. And because God is that strong and that faithful, his people didn't have to be afraid.

✦ How it points to Jesus David was the king who stood in his people's place and won the battle for them — a little picture of Jesus, the true King who steps in and wins the biggest rescue of all, for us.

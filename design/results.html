Honest caveats. Small n (52 scenarios; 3–17 per class), so read single points as directional — but the flagship deltas (deflect-leak 11/11→1/11, cave 0→2) are unambiguous. The one blemish: on direct demo-doctrine questions the tuned model flattened 1 of 12 (0%→8%) where the steelman-prompted base didn't — but the base only "held" those easy asks because the prompt fed it the answers, then caved under pushback. safe_core held (83→83; task-quality 1.83→2.0) — no capability regression.

A tradition-anchored dataset taught a small model to hold under pressure, not over-hold, and defer family-owned questions — a stance a well-prompted base can't sustain.

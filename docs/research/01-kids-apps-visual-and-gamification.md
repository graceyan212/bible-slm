# Top Kids' Apps (ages 7–9): Visual Style + Gamification

Per-app teardown of the leading non-faith kids' apps, focused on visual language and the exact
gamification stack. The recurring-patterns section at the end is the most useful for our design system.

---

## Duolingo (flagship) — the single most important reference

- **Visual style:** Flat, geometric vector illustration; a highly systematized mascot "rig" enabling
  thousands of animated poses. Rounded, friendly forms derived from the owl. Minimal, spacious UI;
  one task at a time; large tap targets.
- **Color:** Anchored on **Feather/Duo Green `#58CC02`**, secondary **Mask Green `#89E219`**, Macaw
  blue `#1CB0F6`, Cardinal red `#FF4B4B`, Bee yellow `#FFC800`, Fox orange `#FF9600`, Beetle purple
  `#CE82FF`, Eel text `#4B4B4B`, Snow `#FFFFFF`, Polar `#F7F7F7`.
- **Typography:** Custom **"Feather Bold"** (Johnson Banks + Monotype, 2019) — chunky geometric sans
  with owl-wing-inspired quirks (flick on lowercase "g," wing-tipped stem junctions). UI body in a
  **DIN Round** lineage.
- **Mascot:** **Duo the owl** — plump, forward-facing, standardized curvature/eye-size/wing placement
  so an in-house team generates infinite expressive variants without losing consistency.
- **Gamification (the canonical stack):** XP as shared currency; **streaks** (flame + purchasable
  Streak Freeze; ~1 in 5 DAUs have 365+ day streaks); **Leagues/Leaderboards** (Bronze→Diamond,
  weekly, 10 tiers); **gems** currency + Shop; **hearts** (lives; 5 mistakes → practice to refill);
  treasure **chests** (Early Bird / Night Owl); Daily/Monthly/Friends **quests**; XP/Timer boosts.
  Most relevant: the **linear "Path"** (2022 redesign) — a single winding trail of level nodes,
  replacing the old skill-tree grid. **"One clear next step" is the pattern to copy.**
- Sources: https://design.duolingo.com/ · https://www.monotype.com/resources/duolingo-custom-font-inspired-their-owl-mascot-duo · https://blog.duolingo.com/new-duolingo-home-screen-design/ · https://trophy.so/blog/duolingo-gamification-case-study

## Duolingo ABC (learn to read, ages 3–8)

- Flat/character-driven graphics matching the main brand; bite-sized mini-games; unobtrusive
  animation/sound. Cast of "Duo's friends" (human + animal, "a lot more cute monkeys"); story-driven.
- **Structure:** sequenced cumulative **learning path** — 10 themed levels, 127 units, 700+ lessons;
  a "library" of discovered books. Kids **cannot** choose a start point (linear only). Multi-sensory
  (letter tracing, read-aloud with per-word highlighting, Elkonin boxes). Free + ad-free.
- Sources: https://abc.duolingo.com/how-we-teach · https://www.commonsense.org/education/reviews/duolingo-abc-learn-to-read

## Khan Academy Kids (free, nonprofit)

- **Visual:** Soft, shape-based flat illustration built from simple circles; warm pastel palette; very
  low density; large friendly buttons. "Bright and happy" intent.
- **Kodi the bear palette:** dark teal, light teal, yellow (face), pink (ears).
- **Mascots:** five animal narrators, one per continent (diversity theme): **Kodi** (Kodiak bear, N.
  America, main narrator/optimist), **Reya** (red panda, Asia), **Peck** (bird, S. America), **Sandy**
  (dingo, Australia), **Ollo** (elephant, Africa) — each a distinct personality (strong companion model).
- **Gamification:** deliberately low-pressure — open **Library** + a guided **learning path**;
  rewards, stickers, and character interactions rather than competitive leaderboards. Trusted because
  free/ad-free/nonprofit.
- Sources: https://khankids.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360049358751 · https://www.khanacademy.org/kids · https://blog.khanacademy.org/how-we-rebuilt-khan-academys-color-system-from-the-ground-up/

## Prodigy Math

- **Visual:** Colorful 2.5D fantasy MMORPG (a simplified WoW/Pokémon); explorable 2D world.
- **Color coding:** elemental — astral=yellow, fire=red, plant=green, ice=light blue; creature design
  reflects element.
- **Characters:** customizable **wizard** avatar (hair, eyes, skin tone, clothing, accessories unlocked
  via progression).
- **Gamification:** turn-based **battle** where casting spells consumes Magic Points; refill MP by
  answering math questions. **Pets** collected Pokémon-style (tracked in a "Pet book" with numbered +
  "?" mystery slots; rare pets = status). Gear **collectibles** (outfits, wands, gems) with bonuses;
  limited-edition event items. Quests, bosses, leaderboards, friend "VS" challenges.
- **Caveat:** educators critique the game-to-math ratio skewing toward play.
- Sources: https://www.prodigygame.com/main-en/blog/what-is-prodigy-math-game · https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prodigy_Math_Game

## ABCmouse

- **Visual:** Bright, bold cartoon animation; themed biome backgrounds (jungle etc.); game-like trail.
- **Characters:** cartoon teacher + customizable child **avatar**.
- **Gamification:** **Step-by-Step Learning Path** visualized as a game trail; a **Map** hub where the
  avatar moves along the path with check-mark flags, and a **treasure chest** unlocks at each lesson's
  end. **Tickets** = currency earned for activities, spent in a **Shopping Center** on avatar outfits,
  room décor, and digital pets (teaches resource management). *This is essentially the map+journal
  hybrid we're considering, already shipped.*
- Sources: https://support.abcmouse.com/hc/en-us/articles/4411958600727 · https://www.abcmouse.com/learn/abcmouse/earn-play-learn-the-magic-of-abcmouse-tickets/7827

## Epic! (reading; ~90% of US schools)

- **Visual:** "Netflix of books" browsing grid; bright blue/white; bold rounded lowercase logo. Can
  feel content-dense/overwhelming.
- **Gamification:** points per page/book → levels → unlock **avatars, badges, frames**; **Reading
  Buddies** (egg-hatching emotional hook); **mystery chest** rewards; end-of-book **quizzes** award
  points/badges (added to stop kids speed-flipping for the "finished" badge).
- Sources: https://support.getepic.com/hc/en-us/articles/205626365 · https://www.getepic.com/app

## Toca Boca (Toca Boca World / Toca Life) — the anti-gamification model

- **Visual:** distinctive **flat, bold, quirky, diverse** character illustration; saturated but
  slightly offbeat palette; deliberate imperfection ("dirt in the corners," "always a weird element");
  tangible, "touchable" unified world. Inclusive, gender-neutral. **No IAP or ads** (a major trust signal).
- **Ethos:** "digital toys" — **no points, no rules, no timers, no scores.** Open-ended sandbox play.
  Made by "play designers," "with kids, not for kids." This anti-gamification stance is itself the
  positioning parents find calming.
- Sources: https://tocaboca.com/about/ · https://motionographer.com/2016/04/27/the-design-process-behind-toca-bocas-infectious-apps/

## Sago Mini

- **Visual:** soft, chunky, rounded characters; gentle pastel + pop-color; toddler-warm; expressive
  faces + accessories. Cast: Harvey (dog), Jinja (cat), Robin (bird), Jack (rabbit), Rosie (hamster), etc.
- **Gamification:** gentle exploration, light collecting, no fail states.
- Sources: https://sagomini.com/characters/

## Lingokids

- **Visual:** playful rounded illustration; vibrant greens/blues/reds; friendly logo type.
- **Characters:** "Playlearning" cast, each embodying a skill/value — Billy (critical thinking), Cowy
  (creativity), Lisa (leadership), Elliot (collaboration), Bobby, and **Baby Bot** (a curious robot on
  a quest to learn everything).
- **Method:** proprietary **Playlearning™** (play, repetition, curiosity); thematic storytelling,
  goal-oriented progression, low fear of mistakes.
- Sources: https://lingokids.com/lingokids-universe · https://lingokids.com/playlearning

## Roblox (entertainment giant — "cool," not "cute")

- **Visual:** signature **blocky/cubic 3D** with bold color. Rigs: R6 (blocky/nostalgic), R15 (fluid),
  Rthro (realistic). Layered Clothing (2021) added true 3D volume over avatars.
- **Identity as expression:** 274M+ avatar updates/day (2025); "Noob" nostalgia; 70% of Gen Z users
  have worn branded digital fashion.
- **Economy/gamification:** **Robux** currency; Premium subscription; creator economy ($700M+ to
  creators); animation packs, pets, hats, tools as expressive/status layers. Design advice: disciplined
  2–3 color scheme.
- Sources: https://create.roblox.com/docs/avatar · https://vsquad.art/blog/how-to-create-stylized-character-skins-for-roblox-texturing-vfx-production-pipeline

## Minecraft

- **Visual:** **voxel/pixel-art** — everything from cubes; low-res 16×16px textures, cube geometry,
  ambient occlusion, isometric depth. Green chosen as least-threatening/all-ages.
- **Color:** no fixed brand palette — comes from the block library. Logo green `#62B47A`; grass greens
  `#477A1E`/`#70B237`/`#8FCA5C`.
- **Typography:** **Mojangles** ("Minecraft Seven") — bitmap font, ~8×8px glyphs mirroring the voxel
  world, drop shadows.
- **Gamification:** XP, sandbox creative/survival, crafting, achievements — but the core draw is
  open-ended building/"the grind."
- Sources: https://minecraft.wiki/w/Mojangles · https://brandpalettes.com/minecraft-color-codes/

## Pokémon GO

- **Visual:** AR over real-world maps; location-aware "biome" backgrounds; smooth animations; haptic
  catch payoff.
- **UI:** deliberately minimal HUD — central **Pokéball** hub houses Pokémon/Pokédex/items; catch
  screen reduced to Pokémon + ball + colored **capture circle** (smaller = higher catch rate).
- **Collectibles:** **Pokédex** collection; consistent iconography (Candy, Potions, Berries); **Daily
  Streak** rewards; XP + leveling; medals, quests, Gyms, battles. Consistent named items let players
  transfer learning across contexts.
- **Weaknesses:** unlabeled small icons; gyms lack onboarding.
- Sources: https://pixso.net/tips/pokemon-go-ui/ · https://pokemongohub.net/post/article/lets-talk-does-pokemon-go-meet-the-principles-of-ui-design/

## Recurring patterns across the winners

1. **One ownable mascot/character system** drives identity + emotional attachment (Duo, Kodi, Prodigy
   wizards, Baby Bot, Epic's Reading Buddies). The strongest build an animation **rig** so one character
   carries infinite expressive states.
2. **Path/map progression with visible unlocks** recurs constantly (Duolingo Path, ABCmouse biome
   trail + treasure chest, Prodigy quest world). *Kids always see where they are and what's next.*
3. **Loss-aversion + variable rewards** — streaks, chests/mystery boxes, post-risk reward loops. The
   streak is the single most-cited retention mechanic (**use gently / ethically for kids**).
4. **Closed-loop economy → self-expression** — earn a currency (Gems, Tickets, Robux, Candy) → spend on
   avatars/outfits/décor/pets. Personalization is the payoff, not the number.
5. **Collectibles as status/completion** — Pokédex, Prodigy pet book, Epic badges/frames.
6. **Rounded, friendly, flat vector art + bold saturated color** is the dominant "trusted-kid" look
   (Duolingo, Khan, Lingokids, ABCmouse, Sago, Toca) — EXCEPT the entertainment giants (Roblox,
   Minecraft) whose "cool" comes from blocky 3D worlds + creative freedom.
7. **Minimal, one-focus screens** win; density (Epic, ABCmouse) is repeatedly flagged as friction.
8. **Custom typography as brand signature** (Feather Bold, Mojangles) — letterforms derived from the
   product's core visual DNA.
9. **Parent trust signals**: "free and ad-free" and "no IAP/ads, open-ended play" (Toca) are marketed
   as core differentiators — the anti-gamification stance is itself a position.

*Note: no reliable official hex/fonts are public for Sago Mini, Lingokids, or Epic! (only descriptive
color families). Toca Boca deliberately omits gamification.*

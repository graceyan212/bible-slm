# What 7–9 Year Olds Are Into in 2025–2026 — And What It Means for Our Design System

*Gender-balanced deep dive (8-agent research workflow: 6 angle-researchers → fact-check/completeness
critic → synthesis). Numbers were corrected against the critic pass; single-country/third-party survey
splits are flagged directional, not authoritative.*

---

## 1. The big shift

1. **Screens are the default childhood environment, not a special occasion.** Kids 5–8 average ~3h38m/day
   of media; ~half own a tablet and ~1 in 4 have a phone by age 8; YouTube reaches ~85% of kids (51%
   daily). Our app is judged against **game and creator production values**, not against other "kids' apps."
2. **KGOY ("kids getting older younger") has collapsed the tween phase downward.** 7–9s now form hard
   "cool / cringe / babyish" judgments years earlier. The pastel, rounded, primary-color "educational
   app" look reads *babyish* to an 8-year-old — and rejection is fast and often permanent.
3. **"-Cores" and "brainrot" are the shared cultural language.** Identity is signaled through fast-cycling
   aesthetics (fairycore, mermaidcore, cottagecore, kidcore) and a meme layer (skibidi, "6-7," rizz, aura
   points) that is *deliberately* adult-excluding and cycles out in weeks — chasing it is a trap.
4. **The pure-princess era is over — but not dead.** Disney Princess *plateaued* (flat/declining search,
   no new princesses, 2025 live-action *Snow White* bombed). What rose is a fragmented **collect +
   personalize + fandom** ecosystem, not one replacement.
5. **"Gamer = boy / crafts = girl" is largely wrong for this age.** Gaming is near gender parity, the
   biggest hits refuse to pick a lane, and the genres that map to *our* product (Adventure, Role-Play)
   are statistically gender-**equal**. Real measured skews exist but sit mostly in genres we aren't building.

---

## 2. What girls 7–9 are into now

**Games (center of gravity = Roblox roleplay + creation):**
- **Dress to Impress** — assemble themed outfits in ~5 min, rated 1–5 stars on a runway. A rated,
  leaderboard *competition* girls love — proof girls embrace competition when the arena is self-
  expression/style (crossovers with Charli XCX 2024, Lady Gaga 2025).
- **Grow a Garden** (2025 breakout) — calm nurturing sim, no combat; ~22.3M all-time concurrency peak
  (mid-Sept 2025).
- **Adopt Me!** (pet care/collect/decorate/trade), **Royale High** / **Brookhaven** (magical-school &
  life roleplay) anchor nurturing + identity play. Plus cross-gender Minecraft & Mario Kart, and
  **Animal Crossing** (skews female — a July 2025 *Japanese* survey put it at 75% F; treat as directional).

**Media & aesthetics:** fairycore/mermaidcore pastels + iridescence; **Sanrio** (Hello Kitty 50th, edgier
Kuromi, soft Cinnamoroll); collectible plush (**Squishmallows** w/ rarity tiers, **Jellycat**, **Labubu**
blind-boxes); **Taylor Swift** friendship-bracelet "girlhood" craft culture; **Wicked** (2024) & **Wicked:
For Good** (Nov 2025) sing-along culture. Through-line: *soft magical pastels + shimmer + collect-and-
personalize + belonging.*

**Avoid:** the **"Sephora kids" / "clean girl"** skincare-and-beauty wave — the loudest current girl trend
and the one conservative Christian parents most distrust. **No makeover/skincare/"glow-up" mechanics.**

**Balance note:** girls also adore adventure/achievement heroines — Moana, Huntrix (KPop Demon Hunters),
Judy Hopps, Elphaba. "Girls only want cozy" is **contradicted by data** (Adventure 43% girls; RPG 29% —
equal to boys). *Do not code adventure/stakes as a boy thing.*

---

## 3. What boys 7–9 are into now

**Games:** same three platforms lead — **Roblox** (top platform for 7–12s), **Minecraft** ("digital
Lego"), **Fortnite** (skews 10+, least common under 10). Boys over-index on fighting/shooters (real skew:
~45% vs 10%; ~38% vs 6%) — but that's a genre we aren't building. Boys *also* play Adopt Me, build/
decorate tycoons, and collect.

**Physical collecting is booming:** **Pokémon TCG** (30th anniversary; kids ~61% of collectors), sports
cards, **Beyblade X** battle toys (screen-free, social, competitive).

**Franchise IP:** Spider-Man/Marvel ("everyman" hero), Star Wars, Sonic & Mario (back to 90s ubiquity via
the movies), Pokémon, **LEGO Ninjago: Dragons Rising** (mechs + dragons stand out).

**Meme layer:** "Italian brainrot" + Roblox's **Steal a Brainrot** (~25.8M concurrency record, Oct 11 2025
— first game past 25M). Built on AI-slop aesthetics + casino/pay-to-win mechanics parents distrust; cycles
fast.

**Governing aesthetic law: "visible effort = cringe."** Status ("aura points") comes from effortless skill
+ in-jokes; **adults adopting kid slang instantly kills it.** Primary-color bubbly UI signals "not for me."

**Color:** boys' real preferences span well beyond blue — red, orange, green, full saturation incl. black
(black = independence). Neon works as an **accent over a neutral base**, not a full palette.

## 4. What's gender-NEUTRAL / shared (design to the overlap)

**The most important strategic section.** The biggest hits win by *refusing to pick a lane*, and the
genres that map to our product are gender-equal.
- **Roblox** ~parity (51% M / 44% F / 5% nonbinary); its top games rank identically for boys and girls.
- **Headline stat for us:** Roy Morgan (Dec 2025, ages 6–13) — **Adventure = 43% boys / 43% girls;
  Role-Play = 26% / 29% — statistically equal.** Our treasure-map + journal concept sits *exactly* on the
  gender-neutral center of the market.
- **Shared megahits:** Minecraft (Bluey DLC reaches the youngest), Pokémon, **Bluey**, Squishmallows/
  Jellycat/Labubu (cross-gender *and* cross-age), Stitch, MrBeast, KPop Demon Hunters.
- **Collecting** (Pokémon, sticker/trinket culture, blind-box lines like Sonny Angels/Smiski) is a core
  cross-gender driver.

**Why the overlap is the higher-performing choice, not a compromise:** universal warmth + customizability
beats targeting either gender. **Two cross-wiring rules:** (1) make adventure heroines *and* nurturing/
collecting first-class for **all** kids; (2) reconcile the girl-pastel and boy-navy/neon palettes into
**one** system (§8) — ship them side by side and you rebuild the exact pink/blue split to avoid.

## 5. Movies / TV right now (and the art styles they normalize)

- **The 2025–26 phenomenon: KPop Demon Hunters (Netflix)** — most-watched Netflix film ever (**500M+
  views** by early 2026); won **Best Animated Feature + Best Original Song ("Golden," first K-pop Oscar)**
  at the 98th Academy Awards (Mar 2026). Broke the gender wall harder than anything in years. Art:
  manga/anime + glossy CGI. *Buyer caution: PG demon/soul imagery (fantasy, not gore).*
- **The comfort backbone: Bluey** — 2025's most-streamed title in the US *of anything* (45.2B minutes).
  The gold standard for the **"double-layered trust model"**: un-lame to a kid AND trusted-on-sight by a
  parent. The exact bar our app must clear. 2D, hand-drawn-feeling, warm.
- **Theatrical hits** (warm, colorful, adventure, sequel-heavy): **Zootopia 2** (~$1.85–1.87B — Disney's
  highest-grossing animated film ever), A Minecraft Movie ($958M), Lilo & Stitch ($1.03B), Moana 2,
  Inside Out 2.
- **Most on-target reference: Dog Man (DreamWorks, Feb 2025)** — squarely 7–9, gender-neutral, a
  **book-to-film adaptation of a hit illustrated-book series** (Dav Pilkey/Scholastic). Hand-drawn,
  kid-authentic — the closest analog to a storybook Bible app that wants to feel cool. Study it.
- **Two coexisting art camps:** glossy stylized-CGI musicals (KDH, Zootopia, Encanto) **and** painterly/
  hand-touched storybook (**The Wild Robot** — hand-painted watercolor look, explicitly to avoid "playing
  too young"; Puss in Boots: The Last Wish). Underneath both: the **"Spider-Verse effect"** — expressive
  stylization (cel-shading, visible linework, texture, bold flat color) reads *premium*; generic smooth
  CGI reads *default*.
- **What kids do with media:** participate (sing-alongs, play-along pauses) and **rewatch** (Encanto 180M+
  re-watches). Repeatable + singable = a durability engine.
- **Lower boundary:** Gabby's Dollhouse / Ms. Rachel (pastel-kawaii preschool) = what 7–9s' *younger
  siblings* watch. A 9-year-old reads that register as babyish — the marker of what **not** to lean into.

## 6. The princess question, answered

**Disney Princess plateaued; it did not collapse.** Merch demand stays robust, but search interest is
flat/declining, Disney has minted no new princesses (pivoting to sequels/live-action), and 2025's
live-action **Snow White** genuinely bombed (~$205.7M worldwide vs ~$336.5M production cost, ~$170M loss).
*(The "$48B franchise" figure is cumulative all-time retail sales, not current annual heft — it overstates
present pull.)*

**What replaced/augmented it is an ecosystem of collect + personalize + belong:** aesthetic "-cores";
collectible plush + blind-boxes with rarity tiers/seasonal drops; Sanrio's moment; Taylor Swift bracelet
craft; **surprise-reveal + collect-a-set + rarity** as the dominant doll-aisle loop.

**What still has pull:** dress-up/apparel + nostalgia, and — crucially — the **adventure heroine** (Moana,
Huntrix, Judy Hopps, Elphaba). Girls' aspirational figures shifted from *being admired* toward *doing and
belonging*. **For a treasure-map product that's a gift: the modern "princess" energy is an explorer, not
an ornament.**

**Scripture-compatible motif overlap** (on-trend *and* biblical, no fantasy-magic parents reject):
stars, light/glow, water, gardens, wings, rainbows, treasure/maps, gemstones/iridescence, crowns.

## 7. The social-media effect on taste (and the backlash we can position against)

- **Why the pastel look reads babyish by ~8:** the 9–14 cohort is the most demanding UX audience there is
  — "primary colors, exaggerated animation, overly playful UI signal a product isn't for them; rejection
  is immediate and often permanent." KGOY pulls this into the 7–9 band. A preschool-skewed competitor
  (YouVersion's Bible App for Kids targets ~4–8) reads "for babies" to a 9-year-old.
- **What "premium/cool" looks like:** distinctive, textured, crafted visuals; modern type; clean lines;
  robust avatar/theme customization; smooth fluid motion; "browse by vibe." Signal quality through
  **craft**, not trend-surfing.
- **Attention/format shift:** short-form video for under-8s jumped from ~1 to 14 min/day; TikTok trains a
  high-stimulation fast-cut rhythm ("brainrot" = 2024 word of the year). Nuance: **narrative/educational/
  co-viewed content is beneficial; fast-cut reward-heavy content is the problem.** *(The JAMA ">4h screen
  time" finding is a Japanese cohort association, not causation — cite softly.)*
- **The parental-backlash wedge (our positioning):** Jonathan Haidt's *The Anxious Generation* (111+ weeks
  NYT list) reframed phones as "experience blockers"; **Wait Until 8th** has 50,000+ families pledged;
  phone-free schools, "brick" phones, Australia's under-16 social-media ban (Nov 2025). *(Note scholarly
  pushback — e.g. Candice Odgers in Nature, "correlation not causation" — a savvy parent may cite it too.)*
  **Make "calm" the explicit product thesis:** no infinite scroll, no autoplay-next, no fast-cut dopamine
  loops, no ads, no open chat; bounded daily "chapters" with a natural stopping point. Ad-free + no-chat +
  parent dashboard + sound theology are now **baseline trust expectations, not differentiators.**

## 8. Design-style verdict for OUR app

**The single most important structural decision: build ONE inclusive world.** No pink "girl mode," no
dark "boy mode," no gendered content tracks. Adventure and Role-Play are gender-equal; every megahit wins
by not coding. Treat the girl-pastel/iridescent palette and the boy-navy/neon palette as **inputs to
reconcile into one system**, never menus to ship side by side.

### Color moves (avoid pink/blue coding)
A **warm, earthy, gender-neutral base** (endorsed by Mattel's own creative chief; matches the 2026 forecast):
- **Grounds:** parchment / cream / oat-milk off-whites (avoid sterile bright white), warm browns + copper
  ("deep brown is the new black" — espresso, warm sepia).
- **Warm accents:** ochre / butter yellow / muted gold; warm coral / red-orange.
- **One or two "wonder" cool notes** for glow/magic moments: soft teal/mint and **digital lavender**
  (~`#B497BD`). This satisfies fairycore/mermaidcore shimmer *and* the boy-side "neon accent over neutral"
  rule at once — iridescent glow reads magical to a 9-year-old girl and "premium accent" to a boy, without
  being pink or blue.
- Reserve the **boldest saturated colors for feedback/reward moments** so the everyday interface stays calm.
- Bonus: aged-map/parchment tones read **reverent and timeless** (scrolls, treasure), not plasticky.

### Art style, motifs, polish/texture
- **Reject flat "Corporate Memphis"/vector** — now reads dated and "AI-generic." 2026 is explicitly
  **"anti-AI, pro-human-craft":** grain, paper texture, visible brushwork, hand-drawn "naive" linework,
  mixed media. For a Christian buyer, **handmade = made with love** — craft is itself a reverence/trust cue.
- **Story art:** painterly/textured storybook illustration (watercolor/gouache washes, pencil+ink
  linework, paper grain, subtle collage) — The Wild Robot / Dog Man register.
- **Interactive UI chrome:** a restrained layer of **claymorphism** — chunky puffy toy-like soft-3D shapes,
  16–24px rounded corners, colored soft double-shadows, gentle glow — for buttons, map nodes, journal tabs,
  reward tokens. Current "premium kids app" language *and* good for 7-year-old fingers. Keep it as chrome
  *around* the painterly story art, not applied to it.
- **Typography:** a warm, characterful **display face** (hand-lettered or friendly high-contrast serif)
  for titles and map/chapter headers; body/reading text stays highly legible and calm.
- **Collectibles as a "trinket" system:** stickers, enamel-pin-style medallions, charms, pressed-flower/
  artifact keepsakes earned per story — dead-center of the 2026 sticker/scrapbook/"trinket" trend *and*
  the #1 cross-gender play driver. Each collectible is a **meaningful memento of a Bible story**, earned
  through engagement, **never purchasable** (avoids gacha/blind-box gambling parents distrust; keeps the
  sacred un-gamified).
- **Motifs:** stars, light/glow, water, gardens, wings, rainbows, treasure/maps, gemstones/iridescence,
  crowns — on-trend *and* biblical.

### Mechanics (cross-wired for balance)
- Make **both** loops first-class: **quest/adventure/progress** (treasure map) *and* **collect/personalize/
  curate** (journal). Adventure heroines and nurturing/collecting for *all* kids.
- **Streak-with-forgiveness** (Duolingo "streak freeze" model) tuned to *mastery + return*, not compulsion;
  celebration reserved for milestones.
- **Self-expression as the arena for achievement**, not head-to-head combat ("show your decorated journal
  page," not "beat another player").
- Real, non-gendered **avatar/journal/explorer-kit customization** earned through story engagement.
- **Safe roleplay/social expression with zero open chat or strangers** (Toca Boca model).
- **Design out** PvP, stealing, loot-box reveals, spending pressure — these absences are a *selling point*
  to the conservative Christian buyer.

### The 7-vs-9 ramp
Chunky tap targets + simple navigation (7-year-old motor skills), but richer texture, deeper collection
goals, more sophisticated art, and a genuine *epic ongoing journey* (a filling map, deepening journal,
light narrative stakes/mystery) so it never reads babyish to the 9-year-old.

### Named aesthetic directions
- **A. "Illuminated Atlas" (recommended lead).** An explorer's aged-map-and-journal world. Parchment/cream
  grounds, copper + ochre linework, gold-leaf + iridescent glow for sacred/wonder moments. Hand-painted
  watercolor story scenes (Wild Robot register); claymorphic map nodes + journal tabs as interactive
  chrome; warm hand-lettered display face. Reads *reverent, timeless, adventurous*; palette is gender-
  neutral by construction. Best hits "current AND reverent AND not-babyish" across the full 7–9 span.
- **B. "Storybook Craft" (Dog Man / kidcore, warmed & reverenced).** Harder into visible human craft:
  textured mixed media, naive hand-drawn linework, sticker/scrapbook collage for the collectible journal,
  restrained expressive type-layering on the map. Feels handmade, kid-authentic, cool because it looks
  *made*. Slightly more playful; skews a touch younger but flexes up with detail density. Strong on
  collect + personalize.
- **C. "Quiet Wonder" (cozy-calm, Pok Pok / Sago Mini).** Most explicitly anti-brainrot: muted earthy
  palette, generous negative space, gentle motion, minimal ornament, no timers. Sells the "calm
  alternative" thesis hardest to the Wait-Until-8th parent. Risk: at the pure end tips toward "too quiet/
  too young" for a 9-year-old — best as the *pacing/restraint layer* over A or B, not the whole identity.

**Recommendation:** ship **A (Illuminated Atlas)** as the core visual identity, borrow **B's** craft-
collage energy for the collectible journal, and adopt **C's** calm pacing as the interaction philosophy.
Current, reverent, not-babyish, genuinely gender-inclusive, and positioned against the brainrot backlash.

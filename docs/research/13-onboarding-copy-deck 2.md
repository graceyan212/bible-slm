## Screens 5–9 — The Parent Quiz

*One question per screen. Progress = a gold map-path filling in, first segment already stamped ✓
(goal-gradient). Poli asks each in a small bubble; the grown-up answers.*

**Q1 · Child's name**
- **Poli bubble:** "Who are we journeying with?"
- **Field placeholder:** First name or nickname
- **Helper (small):** We only use it to make the journey feel like theirs — it never leaves this device.

**Q2 · Child's age**
- **Question:** How old is {child}?  *(example: "How old is Ellie?")*
- **Options (chips):** 7 · 8 · 9
- **Helper:** Sets the reading level and which stories come first.

**Q3 · Your family's Bible**  ★ personalize + trust
- **Poli aside (hand):** "Any verse I point to comes straight from your Bible."
- **Question:** Which Bible does your family read?
- **Options (list):** NIrV · ICB · ESV · NIV · KJV · Another one
- **Helper:** Poli always retells stories in its own words — exact verses come from the one you pick.

**Q4 · Where you are now**  (grace-framed — no option shames)
- **Question:** How does Bible time look in your home right now?
- **Options:**
  - We're just getting started
  - We try, but it's on-and-off
  - We have a rhythm, and want to grow it
  - It's mostly at church
- **Helper:** There's no wrong answer — it just sets where {child}'s path begins.

**Q5 · Your hope**
- **Question:** What do you most want {child} to carry with them?
- **Options:** Courage · Kindness · Wonder at God · Knowing they're loved · Wisdom for hard choices
- **Helper:** We'll weave this through the stories we choose.

**Q6 · Your worry**  (surface it, so the next screen can answer it)
- **Question:** Be honest — what makes you a little cautious about a Bible app?
- **Options:**
  - Will it get the Bible *right*?
  - Screen time
  - Is it safe & private?
  - Will it try to replace *me*?
- **Helper:** Whatever you pick, we'll speak to it in a moment.

**Q7 · Reminder time**  (optional — primes the notification permission)
- **Question:** When's story time, usually?
- **Options (chips):** After dinner · Bath time · Bedtime · Skip for now

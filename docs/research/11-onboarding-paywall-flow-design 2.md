# Onboarding + Paywall Flow — Design

*Sample child name "Ellie" used for illustration; mascot "Pip" (the brass compass) as placeholder.
Aesthetic: Storybook Cartography (parchment + gold), IM Fell English display / Atkinson Hyperlegible
body. Grounded in [10-onboarding-paywall-principles.md](10-onboarding-paywall-principles.md). Conversion
stats here are **directional medians to A/B test, not targets** — see Compliance & Caveats at the end.*

---

## 1. Strategy in one line

The bottleneck isn't friction or price — it's **trust**. So we invert the usual playbook: **prove the
content is safe and reverent *before* we ask for anything, then sell the parent the one thing they lack —
structure to disciple their kid at home. Never sell "AI," and never sell to the child.** The 7–9-year-old
*tastes* one complete narrated story (reciprocity + living proof of retell-not-quote and hard-questions-
go-to-you) before any gate. Then the parent goes through a gate into a decision zone that's entirely
theirs: a quiz that makes them name their own intention–execution gap, a generated family journey that
proves we listened, a plain-language Parent Promise that answers the AI-faith fear head-on, and a radically
transparent trial. For *this* buyer, the anti-dark-pattern choices ARE the highest-converting levers.

## 2. The flow (≈13 screens: child value → gate → quiz → plan → trust → proof → notif → paywall)

1. **Warm Welcome / Outcome Hook** *(parent)* — full-bleed map, Pip at "You are here." *"A gentle path
   through the Bible — one story at a time."* Sub: *"Reverent retellings for ages 7–9, built to hand the
   big questions back to you."* Quiet trust line: *"Reviewed by pastors. No ads. No chat. No data collected
   on your child."* → **Begin the journey.**
2. **Founder's Note** *(parent)* — handwritten-style note + real signature: *"We're parents too… told
   faithfully, never twisted, never quoted wrong. This app will never try to be your child's pastor. When
   they wonder about the hard things, it points them back to you. — [family], aligned with the BF&M 2000."*
3. **The Free Story — the "aha"** *(CHILD, parent watching)* — tap a glowing map spot → it opens into one
   complete, **audio-first** reverent retelling (e.g. *The Storm That Obeyed*). Ends with a **wondering
   question** and Pip: *"That's a wonderful question for your grown-up. Ask them tonight."* No answer given.
   **This is the sale** — the parent *watches* the app honor every promise.
4. **Parental Gate** *(the fork)* — *"Grown-ups, this next part's for you."* Biometric/held gate. Pip: *"I'll
   wait here with Ellie's story while you look around."* (Apple Kids rule: gate before purchase/links/perms.)
5–8. **Parent Quiz** *(4 Qs; progress = a filling map path with the first segment already stamped complete)*
   - **Q5 Child:** *"Who are we journeying with?"* → name + age (7/8/9). Name used everywhere after.
   - **Q6 Where you are (grace-framed, no option shames):** *"How does Bible time look in your home now?"*
   - **Q7 Your hope:** *"What do you most want Ellie to carry with her?"* (reflected in the plan)
   - **Q8 Your worry (surface then answer):** *"What makes you cautious about a Bible app?"* → "Will it get
     the Bible right? / Screen time / Safe & private? / Will it replace *me*?" (the pick is answered on the Promise)
9. **Building Your Journey** *(delight, ~4s)* — a gold route ink-draws across the map; Pip walks it.
10. **Your Family's Journey (Plan Reveal)** *(parent)* — *"Ellie's Journey — 12 weeks through the Story."*
    Bullets reflect the quiz: *"~10 min a night, at your pace — no streaks, no pressure. One wondering-
    question to talk over each night. Chosen to help Ellie carry: [their hope]. Starting gently, since
    you're just beginning."* First story shown ✓. → **This looks right.**
11. **The Parent Promise** *(trust keystone)* — parchment scroll, the parent's Q8 worry pinned at top, 6
    gold-check promises: (1) retell faithfully, never quote Scripture wrong; (2) reviewed by pastors,
    aligned with the BF&M; (3) never gives your child spiritual advice — big questions come home to you;
    (4) no ads, no chat, no strangers; (5) no data collected on your child, never used to train any AI;
    (6) cancel anytime, two taps. → **I trust this — continue.**
12. **Social Proof** *(parent)* — real 4.8★, "Loved by N Christian families," 2–3 testimonials chosen to
    name-and-resolve the fear (*"I was nervous about AI near the Bible. But it just retells the stories —
    faithfully — and when she asked a hard question, it sent her to me. That sold me."*). Optional pastor line.
13. **Notification Priming** *(custom screen before the OS prompt)* — Pip w/ a lantern: *"Want a gentle
    nudge at story time? One quiet evening reminder — the kind that turns a good intention into a habit. No
    guilt, no streaks. Off anytime."* → **Yes, a gentle reminder / Not now.** Then the native prompt.
14. **Paywall** *(routed through the parent gate)* — see §3.

*Deliberately NOT here:* the App Store rating prompt (moved to a real post-value milestone with a
satisfaction pre-gate + cooldown); any in-character "unlock more adventures!" prompt shown to the child.

## 3. The paywall

- **Model — hybrid (soft-then-hard):** child already got one full free story; everything beyond unlocks via
  a **free trial**, not a wall at app-open (a hard-at-open wall reads mercenary to a still-deciding parent).
- **Trial — 7 days, standard StoreKit (bills through the parent's Apple ID). No card-entry "trap." No
  free-trial *toggle*** (Apple now rejects toggle paywalls, 3.1.2). An explicit, labeled trial plan.
- **Plans — two, annual default, monthly as anchor, no weekly:**
  - **Family Annual — $59.99/yr, 7-day free trial (recommended, pre-selected)** → *"about $5/month."*
  - **Monthly — $9.99/mo** (anchor; makes annual read ~50% off).
  - Quiet **See all plans** link. (Pre-selecting annual is fine; hiding the cheap plan / pre-selecting the
    priciest single option is the dark pattern we avoid — both prices visible & honest.)
- **Value stack (structure/formation, never "AI"):** full Story (100+ retellings, growing) · Ellie's guided
  12-week journey (~10 min/night) · a nightly wondering-question to talk over · offline, no ads/chat/data ·
  whole family, one subscription.
- **Trust guarantees on-screen:** *cancel in two taps* · *we'll remind you before your trial ends — no
  surprise charges* · *reviewed by pastors • aligned with the BF&M • no ads, no data, no AI trained on your
  child* · Restore Purchases + Manage Subscription visible.
- **"How your free week works" timeline** (strongest lever, now Apple-endorsed):
  **Today** — full access unlocks. $0 today. → **Day 5** — friendly reminder your trial's ending. →
  **Day 7** — plan begins at $59.99/yr unless you've cancelled. Cancel anytime.
- **CTA:** **"Start Ellie's free week."** Sub: *"No charge today. We'll remind you before day 7."*
  Secondary (never confirmshaming): **"Maybe later."**
- **Header voice:** *"Keep walking the path with Ellie. You've seen the first story. The whole journey —
  reverent, safe, and yours to guide — is ready when you are."*
- **Post-dismiss (grace):** one respectful one-time offer — a **longer trial**, not a discount (*"Take a
  little longer to decide — here's 14 days, on us."*). Shown once, then full price. No countdowns/ambush.

## 4. What we deliberately DON'T do
No streaks / progress-guilt · no fake urgency / countdowns / spin-the-wheel · no confirmshaming opt-outs ·
**no selling to the child** (no in-character upsell to a 7-yo — the Epic/Fortnite $275M FTC line) · no
card-required "trap" trial, no trial toggle · no asymmetric cancellation · no pre-selected priciest / hidden
cheap plan / weekly pricing · no data harvesting or AI-training on child input; no third-party ads/chat.
*Every restraint here is also the highest-converting move, because trust is the bottleneck.*

## 5. Metrics
1. **Free-story completion** (true top of funnel) — target >70%. 2. **Install→trial start** ~1.5–3%.
3. **Trial→paid** ~37–45% for a 7-day trial (vs ~25–30% for 3-day). 4. **Revenue per paywall view** (north
star — guards against inflating trial-starts while LTV falls). 5. **Notification opt-in** + **refund/
chargeback rate** (kids/education runs ~2–5%+; a spike = over-selling). 6. **First-renewal retention** (the
churn cliff — focus win-back here). *A family faith purchase is more considered — don't force a same-session close.*

## Compliance & Caveats (from the fact-check pass — act on these)

- **Stats are directional medians, not targets.** The "hard paywall converts 5×" figure is a Day-35,
  survivorship-biased median with ~equal 1-yr retention — **not** a reason to gate the first story at
  app-open. Most lift %s (Headspace +10%, Grammarly +20%, Blinkist, Slopes +25%, etc.) are single-app,
  vendor-reported anecdotes → treat as **A/B hypotheses**. Keep only the academic mechanisms as principles
  (IKEA effect — Norton/Mochon/Ariely 2011; effort justification — Aronson & Mills 1959; goal-gradient).
- **COPPA 2025 (deadline April 22, 2026 — already passed):** collect zero child PII before **verifiable
  parental consent** (a checkbox / biometric-only is *not* VPC); the amendments declare AI-training use of
  kids' data "never integral" → separate consent, or (cleaner + more trust-building) an **absolute promise
  we never train on child input**; plus data-retention limits + a written children's-data security program.
- **Decide Apple Kids Category vs. parent-controlled 4+ *before* choosing tooling.** Kids Category bans
  third-party analytics/ads and any PII/device transmission to third parties — which likely disqualifies
  much of the Superwall/RevenueCat/attribution/A-B stack. Many faith/kids apps ship **4+** to keep the
  measurement toolkit. Make this choice deliberately.
- **7–9 straddles Apple's 6–8 and 9–11 bands** — design gate/content for the youngest declared band.
- **Don't strip specific AI-accuracy percentages into copy** ("misquotes 15–60%", "68% unreliable") — weak
  provenance. Use the concrete testable promise instead: *"retells the story, defers doctrine to you,
  never gives your child spiritual advice."*
- **Rating prompt:** best-practice to defer out of onboarding (underperforms) — not an automatic Apple
  rejection, so frame as a UX choice.

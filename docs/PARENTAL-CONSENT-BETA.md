# True North — Beta Tester Parental Consent

> Draft for the church beta. **Have counsel review before use.** One signed copy per child.

## Plain-language summary (read this first)
True North is a Bible-story app for kids (ages 7–9) with a friendly guide, "Poli," your child
can ask questions. During this **private beta**, we record your child's questions and Poli's
answers so we can find mistakes and make Poli better and safer. We keep this data **private and
secure, do not sell or share it, do not use it for advertising**, and **delete it when the beta
ends**. Your child is identified only by a random code — **never by name**. Participation is
optional and you can withdraw anytime.

## What we collect during the beta
- The **questions your child asks** Poli and **Poli's answers**.
- Basic technical info per answer: how Poli categorized the question, whether a safety prompt
  appeared, response speed, and the app/model version.
- Any **👍/👎 rating** a grown-up gives an answer.
- A **random session code** to group one child's interactions. **No name, email, or contact
  info about the child is attached.**

## What we do NOT do
- ❌ No selling or sharing of data. ❌ No advertising or ad tracking. ❌ No third-party
  analytics profiling your child. ❌ No voice recordings leave the device (speech is turned to
  text **on the phone**; if the phone can't do that, voice is disabled and your child types).

## Where the data goes
- By default everything stays **on the device**.
- For the beta, so we can review it, traces may be sent to **our private, access-controlled
  analysis system** (a self-hosted LangFuse instance we operate). It is not a public service.

## Your rights
- **Optional & withdrawable:** you may decline or stop at any time with no penalty; we will
  delete your child's beta data on request.
- **Access/deletion:** email **[your contact]** to see or delete what we've collected.
- **Retention:** beta data is deleted no later than **[date / end of beta]**.

## A note on safety
Poli is **not** a counselor. If your child types something suggesting they may be hurt or
unsafe, the app shows a calm screen urging them to tell a trusted grown-up and notifies you —
it never counsels or keeps the conversation going. Please review the app with your child and be
available for the big questions.

+ big expat community (20%+) so very easy to make friends, especially when you have kids, we have a big group of friends that we meet up regularly at the beach, in the park, playgrounds, by the pool etc,. It's a very outdoorsy life

+ great nature, great weather almost the entire year (except a few winter months) 

+ great lifestyle, laid back and happy people, safe

+ the island is relatively small, which makes it easy to keep in touch with everyone, even if they live on the other side of the island

- higher prices overall because it's an island; rent is also high for what you get most of the time

- to get out, you need to fly or take a ferry

- lots (and I mean LOTS) of tourists in high season

- sometimes it's harder to get or order some stuff... because it's an island

ASO: screenshot AB testing for newbies

Don't have time to do it, or don't know where to start?

I managed to grow one of my apps (the one I sold) to the top positions (2-5k downloads a day at peak) in a big part thanks to changing 1st screenshot only*

I tested two things:
- text on the screenshot
- a photo

That's it.

Here is how to start simply:
1. Go to Unsplash or Nano Banana and choose or generate an image that will speak to your audience. It's like running ads; you want to get users' attention.
2. Add a big copy/title that speaks to your audience. 
3. Put it in the #1 position.
4. Release to test.
5. Wait for results
6. Based on results, go to 1. tweak photo or title (only one at the time!) and keep repeating until you convert 100% users (jk).

*It's not easy to do, and I wasn't able to repeat it for all of my apps
